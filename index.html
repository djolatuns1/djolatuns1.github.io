About DJ OLATUNS

Ismail Olatunji Taiwo, professionally known as DJ OLATUNS, is an aspiring DJ with a passion for music, creativity and entertainment. His stage name comes from Olatunji, his name, and represents his identity in the world of tunes.

Easygoing, gentle, respectful and kind, he believes in learning from experience, helping others solve problems and using creativity to make a positive impact. His vision is to keep growing, deliver memorable mixes and build a lasting name in entertainment and technology.
